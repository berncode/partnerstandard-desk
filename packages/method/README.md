# PartnerStandard method content

The partnership method PartnerStandard Desk works with: the lifecycle's stages and steps, the partner types, the 4C and CASO
questions, the call guide, the tips, the MAP's starters, examples, guided steps and sample plan, the example market, the
search prompts and the agents' prompts, the program catalog (benefits, Preferred requirements, the partner types'
descriptions, who invoices, payment models), and the links to the guides they come from.
